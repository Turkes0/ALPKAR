About

ALPKAR was founded with a clear mission: to deliver reliable, efficient, and forward-thinking solutions that empower our partners across industries. While logistics remains a cornerstone of our operations, our expertise extends far beyond freight and warehousing. Today, ALPKAR operates as a multifaceted company offering strategic consulting, industrial procurement, and specialized services for sectors such as defense, textiles, and manufacturing. Backed by a skilled team and a global network, we manage complex operations with precision—from end-to-end supply chain coordination to tailored support for mission-critical and high-value projects.
